# Host Stack — productized AI operations for short-term rentals

**Status:** In development, with a co-founder
**Background:** I've worked as an Owner Success Manager at a short-term rental (STR) management company, so I know the day-to-day work from the inside.

---

## The problem

STR management companies grow by adding properties, but their teams don't grow at the same pace. Hours go to the same work over and over:

- Answering the same guest questions (Wi-Fi, check-in, parking) at all hours
- Pulling numbers from several systems to write monthly owner reports
- Keeping house manuals and listings consistent across a portfolio

Smaller operators also resist paying for expensive software when their revenue doesn't support it.

## The offer I designed

| Tier | What's included |
|---|---|
| **Skill pack** | Installable Claude skills for guest replies, house manuals, and owner updates, plus a setup guide |
| **AI Ops Setup** | Map the operation, build and connect the workflows, test on real properties, train the team |
| **Monthly support** | Tuning and new workflows as the portfolio grows |

## What I've made so far

- **The offer and pricing structure:** a ladder from a self-serve skill pack to done-for-you setup to ongoing support
- **A one-page sales site:** hero, problems, services, process, pricing, FAQ, and booking call-to-action
- **Outreach messages** and a go-to-market plan aimed at management companies with 20+ properties
- **The packaging approach:** each workflow is a Claude skill, so it's repeatable and doesn't depend on custom software

## What this shows

Turning domain knowledge into a structured AI offering: finding repeatable work, designing the service around it, and planning how a team adopts it, not just building the tool.
